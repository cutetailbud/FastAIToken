# 发票与企业合作 / Invoices and Enterprise

- **开票**：登录后台 → 提交工单，即可自主申请开票，支持增值税专用发票、普通发票<br>**Invoices**: log in → submit a ticket to request Chinese VAT special or general invoices
- **企业 SLA**：OpenAI Pro 企业线路、Claude Max 企业线路等专属分组，提供 SLA 保障<br>**Enterprise SLA**: dedicated groups such as OpenAI Pro Enterprise Line and Claude Max Enterprise Line come with SLA guarantees
- **对公打款**：支持企业对公转账充值与合作，详情请通过官网联系客服<br>**Corporate transfer**: corporate bank-transfer top-ups and partnerships are supported — contact support via the website
